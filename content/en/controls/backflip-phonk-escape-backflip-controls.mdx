## Platform-Specific Optimization and Troubleshooting

Whether you are grinding on a touchscreen device, a console, or an elite PC battle station, each hardware profile handles inputs differently. Applying the right configurations eliminates misinputs.

### Platform Optimization Steps

1. **PC Players:** Turn off "Enhance Pointer Precision" in your operating system mouse settings. Inside the game, set your camera sensitivity between 1.5 and 2.2 to prevent wild camera swings during flips.
2. **Mobile Players:** Expand the touch radius of your jump and flip buttons within the interface menu. Using a 3-finger "claw" grip lets you manage camera control, jumping, and rotation simultaneously.
3. **Console Players:** Lower your stick deadzones so micro-adjustments register instantly when centering your flips over narrow beams.

### Troubleshooting Input Issues

| Problem | Root Cause | Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Avatar faces forward during flip** | Directional keys released too early | Hold backward input throughout the entire animation cycle |
| **Double-jump fails to flip** | Input buffer timeout | Tap the secondary jump input faster (within 0.2 seconds) |
| **Overshooting small platforms** | Carrying too much sprint velocity | Release the sprint toggle right before triggering your backflip |
| **Framerate stutter on jumps** | High particle counts from auras | Lower graphics quality to 3 or 4 in the main settings tab |

Yes. Inside the options menu, you can tweak both your camera sensitivity and stunt responsiveness. Adjusting rotational sensitivity prevents your avatar from over-spinning and face-planting into kill bricks.

### Why does my character keep falling short during backflip sequences?
Falling short usually occurs when players fail to carry forward momentum into their backward inputs. Ensure you hit maximum running speed before pulling backward to leap across broad platform gaps.

### How do custom auras affect backflip phonk escape backflip controls?
