## Hardware and Peripheral Optimization

Software timing is only half the battle. Your physical setup impacts how reliably your inputs register during demanding acrobatics. When performing complex tricks on a **backflip phonk escape keyboard** obstacle course, key rollover limits and polling rates dictate whether your commands translate cleanly into the engine.

If your keyboard suffers from ghosting—an issue where simultaneous key presses fail to register—your jump command may be eaten while you hold down directional keys.

| Keyboard Spec / Feature | Ideal Setting / Hardware | Impact on Platforming |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Key Switch Type** | Linear Mechanical (Red / Silver) | Lowest resistance for rapid double-taps |
| **Actuation Point** | 1.0 mm – 1.5 mm | Triggers jumps milliseconds faster |
| **Rollover Rating** | N-Key Rollover (NKRO) | Prevents dropped inputs during simultaneous keys |
| **Polling Rate** | 1000 Hz or higher | Near-zero latency response for frame-perfect flips |
| **Keycap Profile** | Cherry or OEM Profile | Comfortable finger travel across long play sessions |

Upgrading to a keyboard featuring N-Key Rollover ensures that holding sprint, pressing back, and tapping jump all fire at the exact same millisecond. This hardware reliability provides a noticeable consistency boost during intense, high-speed runs.
