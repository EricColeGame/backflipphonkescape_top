### How do I stop my character from ragdolling upon landing?
Ragdolling happens when your character contacts a surface at an angle greater than 45 degrees from vertical. To prevent this, cancel your flip rotation slightly before landing by releasing the directional flip input or tapping the forward pitch key to level your avatar's feet.
